# Inventory Intelligence System

## Business Goal

Improve inventory forecasting and reduce stockouts.

## AI Capabilities

### Forecasting

Predict future inventory demand.

### Anomaly Detection

Detect:

- Sudden spikes
- Unexpected inventory drops

### Summarization

Generate executive reports.

## Inputs

- Sales Data
- Inventory Levels
- Lead Times

## Outputs

- Forecast Reports
- Alerts
- Performance Dashboards
